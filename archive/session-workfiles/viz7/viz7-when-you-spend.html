Decisions for Tarun

1. Which scale opens first

  1. Day, because time of day is the point.
  2. Week.
  3. Month.

2. What counts as late

  1. After 9 pm, until 5 am.
  2. After 10 pm.
  3. After midnight.

3. The sentence at the top

  1. Names the pattern in words, with no scolding: "A fair bit happens after 9 pm."
  2. Names it with a share: "About 1 in 4 purchases is after 9 pm."
  3. No sentence, only the grid.

4. Narrow it to one category

  1. Not in this view. All spending only; a habit's own view is in Spending.
  2. A category filter above the grid.

5. A new student with few days

  1. Draw what exists and say "fills in as you go".
