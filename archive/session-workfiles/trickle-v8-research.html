Design rules for v8

  1. Home shows no money figures by default; at most the top goal %.
  2. Never red on Home, the pay pause, or Savings. Green = on pace, amber = a bit fast.
  3. Numbers appear only after a tap, at payment, or on the Budget tab.
  4. The budget number appears in two places: the pay pause and the Budget tab.
  5. Every flow ends on savings progress.
  6. Two jars: Spending (fixed) and Savings (the rest, automatic).
  7. Income is split by rule and confirmed with one card, with undo. No 'To assign'.
  8. At most one prompt on Home. No badge counts.
  9. Insights shows 3 cards a week; the library sits behind More.
  10. Categories are guessed; people correct, never choose from scratch.
  11. No streaks, points, badges, or leaderboards.
  12. Plain words: To assign → New money · Pools → Spending/Savings · Sweep → Month-end top-up · Cover → Use from… · Carried → Next month starts lighter · Settle → Paid back

Open questions

  1. Pace period: should the Home glow track the week or the month?
  2. Where do balance and income live: Budget, Savings, or a small view in the drawer?
  3. When the budget is empty, should the pause offer 'Take from savings', or only 'Borrow from next week'?
  4. Pay pause strength: always, only over a threshold (e.g. ₹200), or only when pace is amber?
  5. Irregular income (part-time, gifts): all to savings, or top up the budget first?
  6. Splits: keep the full feature behind Actions, or reduce to 'Friends owe you' + Remind?
