Trickle · v9 · Phase 2

Every tile says what it’s worth

v9 keeps v8’s tiles, glow and calm Home. It adds labelled tile units, a Sankey, range and small-buy charts, soft sounds and an optional black & white look. Sample data: seeded student, September 2026.

1 · Tile unit rule

Pick the smallest round unit that fits the cap

Units ladder ₹10 · 50 · 100 · 250 · 500 · 1,000 · 2,500. Cap: 30 tiles on a small card, 50 on a wide card, 30 per budget category. One unit per card. A remainder of 10% or more becomes one partly filled tile, in quarter steps.

2 · Widgets, colour and black & white

Eight key widgets

Left: colour (default). Right: B&W option, where categories use brightness steps plus a pattern (solid, 45° hatch, dots, 135° hatch) and a label. Savings stays the brightest thing on the screen.

3 · Sankey

Where September’s money went

Sources, then pots, then categories and goals. Nodes are stacks of tiles at the card’s unit (₹500). Bands draw left to right, savings last, then the savings chime plays.

1 tile = ₹500 · band width to scale
