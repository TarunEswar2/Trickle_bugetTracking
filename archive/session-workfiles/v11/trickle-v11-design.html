Convergence

What each phase decided

5 · Money + setup

Suggested plan from income, seeded by one "which is most like you?" pick; later months copy last month. 4 screens, 4 taps, about 25 s. Leftover goes to savings with no question.

6 · Layout

Home · Money · Pay · Insights. The v8 Actions tab is gone because every decision is now an inline sheet. Home is a widget grid (ref mood) with the pace glow on top.

7 · Loops

Every loop ends on savings. Two loops need no action (subscriptions, leftover). At most 1 notification a day and 4 a week, quiet 22:00–08:00, no badges.

8 · Words

"Spending money", "Set aside", "Move tiles", "starts ₹200 lighter". Buttons say what happens. Never: over, debt, carried, cover.

Phase 7 · Principles

Fourteen rules for every screen

  1. One decision per screen, default pickedFogg 2009; Madrian & Shea 2001
  2. At most 2 numbers on first view; exact ₹ on tapSweller 1988; NN/g progressive disclosure
  3. One unit forever: ■ = ₹100Neurath, Isotype; v11 Phase 3
  4. Home never shows budget or balanceKarlsson et al. 2009, ostrich effect
  5. Savings first, then spending moneyThaler & Benartzi 2004
  6. Feel the payment: tiles leavePrelec & Loewenstein 1998; Soman 2003
  7. Jars with soft edges: move tiles, total staysThaler 1999, mental accounting
  8. Start with progress already shownNunes & Drèze 2006; Kivetz et al. 2006
  9. Fresh start every week and monthDai, Milkman & Riis 2014
  10. End each period on the saved numberKahneman et al. 1993, peak-end
  11. No red, no debt words, no guiltElliot et al. 2007; Shapiro & Burchell 2012
  12. No streaks, points, badges or countsPolivy & Herman; v11 audit
  13. Calm by default: a glow, not alertsWeiser & Brown 1996
  14. UPI link or manual entry only; data stays on the phonev2 decision; trust interviews
