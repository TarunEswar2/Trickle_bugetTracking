Trickle v11 audit

Nine prototype rounds, v2 to v10, read as one story. Each round fixed the last complaint by adding something new to learn. v11 keeps the calm UX of v8 and replaces every unit system with one tile that always means the same amount.

Why users stayed unhappy

  • Every fix added a new thing to learn: chart forms, widgets, tile units, shapes.
  • Unit instability: a tile meant ₹100, ₹500, 1%, a count, ₹10–2,500, then six shapes.
  • Numbers-first defaults on Home caused anxiety and avoidance.
  • Decision load: sliders, cover sheets, inbox, sweeps, widget curation.
  • Breadth over depth: 44 → 20 widgets while the core loop got less polish.
  • Alarm and debt framing: red, "carried", "over".
  • System language over student language: pools, To assign, breaking change.

Decision log

Keep

  • No budget number on Home; pace as glow + word v8
  • One decision per screen, default preselected v8
  • Pay: payment tiles leave the category v8/v9
  • Income auto-split, one confirm + undo v8
  • Money = Income · Budget · Savings accordion v8
  • Goal waffle with head start v8
  • Repeat buys + "3rd time here" line at pay v9
  • Weekly check-in + story ending "You saved ₹X" v8/v9
  • Key on every visual, exact ₹ on tap v9
  • Ledger invariant validator v7
  • Subscriptions as fixed chunk with due ring v3/v9
  • Plain-language glossary v8
  • Soft sound, reduced motion, B&W with pattern v9
  • "Friends owe you", outside balance v7/v8

Drop

  • Adaptive unit ladder
  • Shape denominations, mixed piles, breaking change
  • Sankey as a main view (Insights deep view at most)
  • 20-widget default board (≤3 visible + library)
  • Range, 24h ring, weekday, size widgets by default
  • Inbox of many action cards
  • Leftover Auto/Manual choice
  • Balance as Money headline
  • Allocation pie + sliders in onboarding
  • 30-tile period strip

Never again

  • More than one tile unit, or a unit that changes by card
  • Shape or denomination systems to memorise
  • A new metaphor per screen
  • Budget, safe-to-spend or balance on Home
  • Red deficits and debt words
  • More than 2 numbers on first view; stat triads
  • Badge counts, "N things need you"
  • Multi-slider allocation, multi-option cover sheets
  • Radar, bubble, scatter, radial arcs
  • Streaks, points, badges
  • SMS or notification scraping
  • Accounting jargon in UI (To assign, pools, sweep)
