Weekly savings

At the end of every week a pop-up appears on Home. It shows the week's grid with what was left, in amber and in rupees. You can move it to savings, choosing the goals, or to next week's budget. A week with nothing left, or overspent, shows boxes filled from the top and a reminder of your goals. Drawn for all six students. Options only. You decide.

Step 1: the pop-up on Home

The week's grid with what was left in amber, how much in rupees, and two ways out: to savings or to next week's budget. Yash-type spent it all and Harsh-type overspent, so their grids are filled from the top, with the alert and their goals.

The amber boxes have turned green, each in the shade of its goal. Tap goals to include or leave out; the money splits equally between those picked, and each row says how much sooner it gets there.
