Weekly savings

At the end of every week a pop-up appears on Home. It shows the week's grid with the money you didn't spend in red. You choose which goals it goes into, the red boxes turn green, and the savings ring updates. Drawn for all six students, including a week with nothing left. Options only. You decide.
