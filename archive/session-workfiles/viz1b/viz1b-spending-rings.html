Spending rings

The nested rings are the main view of the Spending tab. Each category is a ring in its own colour, with no labels on the rings. Tap a category and a full screen opens with its grid, a way to look back up to 12 periods, and its transactions. Drawn for all six students. Tap a row or ring in the demo.
