Savings goals

The Savings page is one segmented ring. Each goal is its own section, sized by its amount and filled in its own green by how much is saved. Below it the goals are plain text rows, each with its ETA. Tap a goal for its months, step through them with the arrows, and scroll down for every day of the month. Options only. You decide.

Decisions for Tarun

1. Which ring?

  1. Half ring (A). A fuel gauge, and not a donut, which keeps it apart from the spending rings.
  2. Full segmented ring (B), like your donut reference.

2. How are the sections sized?

  1. By each goal's target amount, so a ₹30,000 goal gets twice the arc of a ₹15,000 one.
  2. All sections equal, whatever the amount.

3. Months: what does the toggle do?

  1. Moves a white outline through the month boxes and writes that month's amount below.
  2. Shows one month at a time, full screen.

4. Most saved months

  1. A bright halo on the three biggest month boxes, no text.
  2. Halo on one only, the single biggest.
  3. Nothing extra: the glow alone shows it.

5. Day grid scale

  1. Glow scaled to the goal's biggest day ever, so months compare.
  2. Scaled to each month's own biggest day.

6. A goal row

  1. Name, a colour dot that matches its ring section, then "how far · ETA".
  2. No colour dot; the order matches the ring instead.
