Savings goals

The first Savings page is a fuel gauge for all your savings, with a smaller gauge for each goal stacked below it and an ETA at the bottom of every gauge. Tap a goal for its statistics, by month in glowing green and amber boxes or by day. A finished goal gets a celebration screen. Options only. You decide.

The Savings page: two gauge forms

Same page both ways: all savings together on top, one smaller gauge per goal below, a Completed row at the end. Row A is a segmented half ring, from your reference, filling from its lower ends. Row B is half of Viz 1's grid, ten boxes by five, filling from the bottom.

Inside a goal: by month and by day

The goal's statistics. By month: twelve boxes, green for added, amber for taken out, glowing more for bigger amounts. By day: the last twelve weeks, one box a day, same colours.

When a goal is done

The celebration screen, pushed like an intro screen. Then the Completed list. Three students, three different goals.

Try it

Pick a student and a gauge form. Tap Goals to collapse the list, a goal for its statistics, a month or a day for its amount, Completed for the list. "Finish this goal" plays the celebration and moves the goal to Completed.

Decisions for Tarun

1. Which gauge form?

  1. Half ring of segments (A).
  2. Half grid, 10 by 5 boxes (B).

2. What is on top?

  1. One gauge for all savings together, goals stacked below it.
  2. No total: the goals are all equal gauges, nearest finish first.

3. What is the ETA at the bottom?

  1. A month and year ("Mar 2027"), from the last three months' pace.
  2. "About 5 months".

4. A goal with no target date

  1. The ETA is from pace. If you set a date, the statistics also say on pace, a little earlier or a little later. No red.
  2. A date is required when you make the goal.

5. Month and day boxes

  1. No numbers on the boxes; tap one for its amount.
  2. Show the net amount on each month box.
