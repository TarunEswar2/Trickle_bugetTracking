Step 5: how to set the budget

C

Split the balance

One slider. A small "Change" link under the balance lets someone say that not all of it is theirs to plan with (my default).

D

Pick categories

Only the basics are shown: food, travel and other basic needs, already picked where they apply. Anything else is found with the search. If search finds nothing, one tap creates it. The full list of 50 still lives behind the search; it is never shown all at once.

E

Share the weekly budget: equal or manual

Fixed bills come off first (bottom of the grid, taupe), then your categories, then the grey Buffer on top. Equal gives every category the same. Manual is a slider per category, or tap the amount to type it. A slider cannot go past what is left.

E3

Fixed bills, inside the budget screen

Tap "Fixed bills" in the budget screen to open this. With UPI linked, subscriptions found in your payments are listed. With manual tracking, it asks. Either way, about a week's worth of each comes off the weekly budget first.

E4

Try it

Switch between Equal and Manual (Preset appears for "New income"). Drag a slider or type an amount: the grid fills in that colour and the buffer takes what is left.

"No particular budget in mind": Trickle helps

A few questions, then a budget is made with them. Drawn for Yash: how much a week, what matters most, anything paid monthly, then the week, set up. What is left of the balance becomes savings.

H

Step 6: savings and goals

A goal has a name, an amount and a date. The screen says how much to save a month (and about how much a week) to get there. No lateness, no ETA. A goal with no date takes what is left, with a slider.

I

When new income arrives

Trickle never guesses what a payment is. Money comes in only when you add it: in the Income tab, tap Add money and say where it belongs. A friend paying you back ₹200 goes back into a category, not into income. If it is new income, you go through the same split as setup, now with Preset (your last split). Nothing pops up on Home (V11-3).

For you to confirm

What you decided is logged. These are the things you asked me to sort out, drawn as my defaults.

1. The recommended basics

  1. Food, Travel, Phone & data, College & study, Personal care, Other basics.
  2. Fewer, or different ones: tell me.

2. Balance is not all yours

  1. A small "Change" link under the balance so someone can enter the amount that is really for this month.
  2. No link. The balance is the balance.

3. Where added money can go

  1. Three places: new income (starts the split), back into a category, or into the buffer.
  2. Only income and a category.

4. Forgotten PIN

  1. Reset with the phone's own screen lock. Data stays on the phone.
  2. No reset; reinstall.

5. The first week

  1. Prorated by days left, said on the "All set" screen.
  2. The first week starts at the next Monday.

6. Goals need more than savings

  1. A calm screen: "Your goals need ₹X. You set aside ₹Y." with "Save more" or "Change a date".
  2. The amounts are silently scaled down to fit.

7. Savings in the no-budget path

  1. Whatever is left of the balance after the weekly budget and fixed bills.
  2. Ask separately.

8. Overspending lowers savings

  1. When savings are touched, goal amounts shrink in the same proportion and the goal screen shows it.
  2. Choose which goal gives.

9. Fixed-bill and category colours

  1. Fixed bills taupe, buffer grey, the first five categories coloured and the rest peach. Tap a box to read which one.
  2. Pattern the extras instead of one colour.
