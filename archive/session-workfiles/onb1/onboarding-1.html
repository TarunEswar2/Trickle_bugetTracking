Steps 1 to 4

Title, PIN, how money comes in, what the app asks for. Copy and layout are my first drafts for you to react to.

B

Step 5a: split the balance

The linked balance is divided into savings and budget before anything else. The drawn default is a slider on one bar, with both amounts written as you move it. Three balances.

C

Step 5b: how to set the budget

Your two ways in. Picking categories is the main path. "No particular budget" must not feel like a lesser choice.

D

Step 5c: pick categories

The recommended set is already picked. Tap to add or remove. "See all 50" opens the full list (below). A search box and "Create your own" sit at the top. Colours follow the ring colours: the first five are coloured, the rest share one.

E

Step 5d: a weekly amount for each

Each picked category asks "how much are you comfortable spending each week?". The bottom line compares the total with the budget you split off, in words. Harsh's 18 categories go over, to test the wording.

F

Create your own

For something not in the 50. A sheet over the picker: a name, an optional emoji-free icon later, and the weekly amount straight away.

The 50 categories

Drawn as the starting list. Bold ones are the recommended eight. Fixed bills and subscriptions are handled in the Fixed group (V8), so they are not in this list.

For you to decide

1. Title screen

  1. The Trickle name, a small fuel-gauge grid filling slowly, one line, "Get started".
  2. The name alone, no picture.

2. PIN

  1. 4 digits. Fingerprint or face is offered on the permissions step.
  2. 6 digits.

3. Linking or manual

  1. Two equal cards, UPI first, and you can switch later. An Excel/CSV import is a small line (placeholder).
  2. Manual first, linking offered later.
  3. Link only; manual lives in settings.

4. Permissions

  1. Notifications (week-end pop-up, nudges) and fingerprint/face. Nothing else asked.
  2. Also camera, for scanning a UPI QR before paying.

5. Savings split

  1. A slider on one bar, with a few quick stops (0, 10, 20, 30%).
  2. Type a rupee amount for savings.
  3. Quick stops only.

6. Starting savings share

  1. Start at 0 and let you move it, since I should not decide how much you save. (The drawn boards show 10–25% only to show the labels.)
  2. Start at 20%.

7. "No particular budget"

  1. Trickle just tracks. After four weeks it shows your real weekly pattern and asks if you would like a budget from it.
  2. One total weekly amount, no categories.

8. Recommended set

  1. The bold eight in the list below (food, travel, phone, outings, stationery).
  2. Fewer, five.
  3. Different eight: tell me which.

9. Going over in setup

  1. Amber words with the amount ("₹400 more than your weekly budget"). You can still continue.
  2. Block continuing until it fits.
