When new income arrives

Whenever money lands, Trickle asks you to split it the same way as at setup: savings or budget, the weekly sliders, then goals. Each screen starts from what you chose last time. For you to decide

1. Title screen

  1. The Trickle name, a small filling grid, one line.
  2. The name alone.

2. PIN

  1. 4 digits. A mismatch says "Those did not match. Try again." in amber and returns to the first entry.
  2. 6 digits.

3. Linking or manual

  1. Two equal cards, UPI first, switchable later; Excel/CSV import as a small placeholder line.
  2. Manual first.

4. Permissions

  1. Notifications and fingerprint/face only.
  2. Also camera for QR.

5. Savings share start

  1. The slider starts at 0 and you move it. No 10/20/30% buttons. The drawn positions only show the labels.
  2. Starts at 20%.

6. When a category is at its limit

  1. The slider stops and a line says "Nothing left to give. Lower another to free some."
  2. The slider takes from the other categories on its own.

7. Leftover in the weekly grid

  1. Unassigned boxes stay empty and are called "spare". At the end of the week they can be saved (V5).
  2. Leftover is put into one extra "other" category.

8. Savings and goals: how often

  1. The savings amount is set aside each month, so every goal can show a date (ETA).
  2. A one-time amount now; dates only appear once savings repeat.

9. Goals in setup

  1. Each goal asks name, amount and by when; the date is optional and so is having a goal at all. Goals can be added later in Savings.
  2. At least one goal required.

10. "No budget" path

  1. Savings split, then step 6; Trickle just tracks spending, then offers a weekly budget after four weeks.
  2. Savings and goals only, with no later offer.

11. Add a goal

  1. At the bottom, beside Finish, in thumb reach; a large card when there are no goals yet.
  2. A dashed row at the end of the goal list.
  3. A floating round button.

12. New income

  1. A prompt on Home with no amount in it; the split, sliders and goals each start from last time. "Later" asks again at next open.
  2. Auto-apply last time's split and only tell you.

13. When money lands mid-week

  1. The new budget starts at once, shared over the weeks left in the month.
  2. It starts at the next Monday.

14. The 50 names and the recommended eight

  1. As in the list: Canteen & mess, Chai & coffee, Snacks, Bus & metro, Auto & cab, Mobile recharge, Outings, Stationery.
  2. Tell me which to swap.
