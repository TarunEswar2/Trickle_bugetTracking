A

Three kinds of tap

Across all ten visualisations a tap does one of three things.

A spot, box or day: a line under the pictureRepeats and time-pattern spots, savings month and day boxes, fixed-bill days. The amount is written below, the picture stays put and the chosen spot gets a white outline.
A category, habit, goal or purchase: its own screenThe ring's categories, repeat habits, savings goals and history rows open a full screen with the exact figures (V1-11).
The week-end pop-up: a pop-up on HomeThe one place that shows rupees without a tap, because you chose it (V5-8).
B

The standard pattern

The same three steps on three different pictures: before a tap (a hint), after a tap (the amount in a line, a white outline on the spot), and the picture does not move. The pictures are a hotspot calendar, savings month boxes and a fixed-bills calendar.

Still to confirm

1. Grids and rings carry no numbers on the marks

  1. A rule: the scale chip ("1 box = ₹X") is the only figure on a grid screen. Amounts go in a line or on tap.
  2. Allow a number on a mark where it fits.

2. The Savings page

  1. No amounts until a goal is opened. The ring and the rows show how far, and the ETA.
  2. Show the saved amount on each goal row.

3. Over and nothing left

  1. Words first ("gone over"), the amount on a tap, except the week-end pop-up where the over amount is emphasised (V5-10).
  2. Always show the amount with the words.

4. Rows that carry amounts

  1. Lists you scan (history rows, fixed-bill rows, week-vs-last categories) carry amounts, as drawn.
  2. Every list hides amounts until tapped.
