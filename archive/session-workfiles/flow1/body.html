The pay screen flow

You choose what a payment is for, and a goal is chosen the way a category is (F-1): spending a goal's saved money to buy or experience the goal. This board draws the whole path, five ways the "what is it for?" list could look, and the awkward moments: not enough saved, going over, and finishing a goal. Drawn across Vaishak (₹3k, 2 categories, 1 goal), Yash (₹9k, 7, 2) and Harsh (₹12k, 18, 3). Options only; you decide.

For you to decide

1. What does "Pay" do?

  1. Opens your UPI app with the amount filled in, and Trickle records it when it goes through.
  2. Trickle only checks first; you pay in your UPI app yourself, and Trickle matches the payment.
  3. Something else: tell me.

2. Order of the steps

  1. Amount first, then what it is for.
  2. What it is for first, then the amount.

3. The "what is it for?" list

  1. A: one list, goals under a "From savings" heading.
  2. B: one mixed list with green goal rows.
  3. C: two tabs, Budget and Savings.

4. A goal's pay screen

  1. The goal's grid in green, the crumble leaving an amber dashed ghost.
  2. The same orange as a category.

5. Not enough saved for the goal

  1. Offer to take the rest from your buffer (the first step of O-23), or choose another goal.
  2. Block it and say how much is saved.
  3. Let it go through and reduce savings elsewhere.

6. When is a goal finished?

  1. After paying from it, ask "Is Trip done?". Yes completes it and plays the celebration.
  2. Completes automatically when what is saved is used up.
  3. Only when you mark it done in Savings.

7. Money left in a finished goal

  1. Goes back to your free savings, and you can give it to another goal.
  2. Stays with the completed goal.
