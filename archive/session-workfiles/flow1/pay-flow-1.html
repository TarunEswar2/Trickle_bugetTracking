The pay screen flow

You choose what a payment is for, and a goal is chosen the way a category is (F-1): spending a goal's saved money to buy or experience the goal. "How much" and "What is it for" are now one screen, with two tabs, Budget and Savings, and the choices drawn as boxes (F-2). The pay screen itself now handles every overspending and not-enough-money case (F-3), and the path ends on one confirmation screen that shows everything relevant as a success or a failure (F-4). This board draws the path, the pay screen in each case, and the confirmations. Drawn across Vaishak (₹3k, 2 categories, 1 goal), Yash (₹9k, 7, 2) and Harsh (₹12k, 18, 3). Options only; you decide.

The pay screen for a goal

The goal's own grid is the picture: green liquid is what is saved. The payment crumbles out of it and leaves a dashed amber ghost (amber means taken from savings, V4-6). A line under says where the money comes from. Scale chip as always.

D

The pay screen, in every case

Same screen, same grid. Only the words and one small panel change. Over a category the buffer pays first, then the other categories equally, then savings (O-23). The "where it comes from" row opens to show the split. A goal that does not have enough saved follows the same order. A low account balance is a warning, not a block.

E

The confirmation screen

One screen at the end of every payment: what happened, how much, to whom, what it was for, where it came from, when, and the UPI reference. Success is green. Failure is amber, says why, and says that nothing was taken from your budget. Then the goal celebration where it applies.

For you to decide

1. What does "Pay" do?

  1. Opens your UPI app with the amount filled in, and Trickle records it when it goes through.
  2. Trickle only checks first; you pay in your UPI app yourself, and Trickle matches the payment.
  3. Something else: tell me.

2. Boxes on the merged screen

  1. Two columns of boxes with a dot, the name and one word.
  2. Three columns of smaller boxes.

3. A goal's pay screen

  1. The goal's grid in green, the crumble leaving an amber dashed ghost.
  2. The same orange as a category.

4. A payment that fails

  1. The crumble is undone, nothing is taken from the budget or savings, and the screen says why and offers "Try again".
  2. Keep it as pending until you confirm.

5. A payment that is slow

  1. A third state, "Waiting for your bank", before it turns into success or failure.
  2. Only success and failure.

6. What the confirmation shows

  1. Amount, to whom, what it was for, where the money came from, when, and the UPI reference. Amounts are on this screen because it is the record.
  2. Fewer lines; the rest on tap.

7. When is a goal finished?

  1. After paying from it, the confirmation asks "Is Trip done?". Yes plays the celebration.
  2. Completes automatically when what is saved is used up.
  3. Only when you mark it done in Savings.

8. Money left in a finished goal

  1. Goes back to your free savings, and you can give it to another goal.
  2. Stays with the completed goal.
