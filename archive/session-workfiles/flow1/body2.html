The pay screen flow

You choose what a payment is for, and a goal is chosen the way a category is (F-1): spending a goal's saved money to buy or experience the goal. "How much" and "What is it for" are now one screen, with two tabs, Budget and Savings, and the choices drawn as boxes (F-2). This board draws the whole path from there and the awkward moments: not enough saved, going over, and finishing a goal. Drawn across Vaishak (₹3k, 2 categories, 1 goal), Yash (₹9k, 7, 2) and Harsh (₹12k, 18, 3). Options only; you decide.

For you to decide

1. What does "Pay" do?

  1. Opens your UPI app with the amount filled in, and Trickle records it when it goes through.
  2. Trickle only checks first; you pay in your UPI app yourself, and Trickle matches the payment.
  3. Something else: tell me.

2. Boxes on the merged screen

  1. Two columns of boxes, each with a colour dot, the name and one word (or "40% there" for a goal). More scroll; search for long lists.
  2. Three columns of smaller boxes.

3. A goal's pay screen

  1. The goal's grid in green, the crumble leaving an amber dashed ghost.
  2. The same orange as a category.

4. Not enough saved for the goal

  1. Say so on the merged screen, then offer the rest from your buffer (the first step of O-23) or another goal.
  2. Block it and say how much is saved.
  3. Let it go through and reduce savings elsewhere.

5. When is a goal finished?

  1. After paying from it, ask "Is Trip done?". Yes completes it and plays the celebration.
  2. Completes automatically when what is saved is used up.
  3. Only when you mark it done in Savings.

6. Money left in a finished goal

  1. Goes back to your free savings, and you can give it to another goal.
  2. Stays with the completed goal.
