Moving money

You can move money out of savings to somewhere else: another goal, a category, the buffer, or free savings (F-5). This is not paying. Nothing leaves your account; it only changes which pot the money belongs to. Same screen family as the pay screen: an amount, two tabs, boxes. Drawn for Vaishak (₹3k, 1 goal), Yash (₹9k, 2 goals) and Harsh (₹12k, 3 goals).

A

Where it starts

From a goal's own screen: a "Move money" button, beside "Pay with this" (the pay flow). My reading; question 1 asks whether it should also start elsewhere.

B

The move screen, across the pool

"From" at the top, with what it holds. The amount, with the most it can move. Then "To" in two tabs: Budget (the buffer first, then categories) and Savings (free savings first, then the other goals). The goal being emptied is left out of the list.

C

Changing the "From"

Tapping the "From" pill opens the same boxes, so the source can be any pot, not only a goal (question 2).

Awkward moments

E

The confirmation

Same confirmation shape as a payment (F-4): what moved, from where, to where, when, and what it changes. A move cannot fail, so there is only the success.

For you to decide

1. Where "Move money" lives

  1. On a goal's own screen, and in a category's screen for moving money back.
  2. Only on a goal's screen.
  3. Also a button on the Savings tab itself.

2. What can be the source

  1. Any pot: a goal, free savings, the buffer or a category. Savings is where it starts.
  2. Only savings, as you said.

3. Money into a category

  1. Adds to its amount for this week only. A small "Make it every week" option changes its weekly amount.
  2. Always changes the weekly amount.

4. Taking everything out of a goal

  1. Ask "Keep Laptop, with nothing in it?" or "Remove it". Keeping leaves an empty goal.
  2. Remove it automatically.

5. What the confirmation says about dates

  1. Plain words: "Laptop is a month later. Trip is 2 months sooner."
  2. No dates on the confirmation; see them on the goal.

6. How it shows in a goal's month view

  1. An amber box on the day it left, a green box on the day it arrived in another goal (V4-6).
  2. It is not shown, since it is not a payment.
