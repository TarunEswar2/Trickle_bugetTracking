Design system · v16.2

Trickle Night Calm

The look of Trickle for students: dark, quiet and readable. It replaces the neon of the first Night system with softer colour, higher contrast text and one calm motion. Every screen in the mockup is built from the parts on this page.

Principles

Four rules the screens follow

Soothing before striking

No neon. Colour is soft and each hue means one thing. Glow is used for what you have, never for alarms.

Readable first

Body text is at least 16px. Every text colour is 6:1 or better on the background. Secondary text is never below 12px.

One picture, one finding

Each screen states its finding in words, then shows one chart that confirms it. Numbers sit beside the picture, not inside it.

Calm motion

Screens settle in once. Numbers count to their new value. A payment drains the bar. Nothing loops or flashes.

Motion

Settle, count, drain

Settle

On a new screen the first nine blocks rise 10px and fade in, 45ms apart, 420ms each.

Count

The big number counts to its new value over 700ms with an ease-out curve.

Drain

After a payment the green bar shortens over 950ms. The amount that leaves is hatched on the confirm screen first.

One curve for everything: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). All motion stops when the device asks for reduced motion.

Voice

Plain, short, never scolding

Say it first

The finding goes above the chart: "Food took the most." "Fridays are your biggest day."

Name the object

"How many days should this money last?" not "Until when?"

No blame

"A bit more than you thought." "Nothing wrong here." Limits are nudges and say so.

Words we avoid

plan, split, pace, buffer, budget, cascade, box. Use allowance, kept, spending fast, left.
