Motion & Sound Lab

Pick a moment to watch it in three motion languages and the recommended mix at once (silent). Press Replay on a stage to hear it, or Play all four to hear them one after another. Each stage has its own sound palette, synthesised live with WebAudio (no files, every voice ≤0.15, compressor + limiter). Sound starts after your first tap. Haptic patterns fire through navigator.vibrate on Android browsers and are logged under each stage everywhere else.

Mixing, defaults and performance

Mixing rules (what this lab runs)

  • One sound per 300 ms. A second sound inside the window is dropped unless it has a higher priority; then the earlier one is ducked to 30% over 40 ms.
  • Priority: goal complete, kept ₹ > pay, income, split, milestone, sweep > snap, question, splash > zoom, sheet, card, drop > press, tab, draw, glow.
  • Every voice peaks at ≤0.15; master 0.9 → compressor (−24 dB, 4:1) → limiter (−3 dB, 20:1) → speakers.
  • Lowpass at 5 kHz on the master so coin partials never get sharp.
  • Audio context opens on the first tap (browsers block earlier sound). Nothing plays at app open before a tap.
  • Mute switch in app settings; phone on silent / Do Not Disturb = no sound, haptics still allowed (Android ringer mode via the native shell).

Performance + reduced motion

  • Only transform and opacity animate (plus SVG stroke-dashoffset for rings and ribbons). No layout properties.
  • Web Animations API; springs are pre-sampled into CSS linear(), so no per-frame JavaScript.
  • Max 30 animated marks per moment (matches the ≤30 marks per card rule); pay drops ≤4 clones.
  • Each sound builds a few nodes and stops them within 1 s; one shared 1 s noise buffer.
  • Pay sequence ≤1.2 s in every language (Phase 10 check S8).
  • Reduced motion (OS setting or in-app toggle): every animation runs at 0 ms, glow never moves, story cards cut; sounds and haptics keep their meaning.

Recommendation

Motion: Calm as the base, Tactile at the moments your finger causes. Calm covers everything the app shows on its own (splash, income arriving, glow, charts, month story, goal milestones). Tactile springs come in only where the student acts: pressing, paying, crumbs snapping, tap-zoom and sheets. Playful is out: it reads as a game, and the brief says no games, no streaks, no confetti.

Sound: a hybrid palette. Coin plinks only at pay and when crumbs snap into a dot, so paying feels a little like handing over cash. Soft chimes for good news (income, savings, milestones, the bangle closing, "You kept ₹X"). Quiet wooden clicks for interface touches. Charts, glow and story cards stay silent.

  • Calm because money anxiety leads to avoidance: people open finance apps less when the news may be bad (Olafsson & Pagel 2018; Shapiro & Burchell 2012). The app has to be safe to open, so nothing jumps or alarms. Calm tech keeps Home in the periphery (Weiser & Brown 1995).
  • Tactile at pay supports the pain-of-paying re-coupling (Prelec & Loewenstein 1998; Raghubir & Srivastava 2008): dots dropping with weight, plus a soft coin sound, make a UPI tap feel like cash leaving. It stays soft and never sounds like a mistake.
  • Chimes for good news line up with the peak-end rule from Phase 7: the session ends on savings, and only savings gets the warm sound.
  • The empty-jar question uses a neutral wooden knock and no shake, so it doesn't read as an error or as blame.

The fourth stage ("Recommended") plays this mix for every moment.
