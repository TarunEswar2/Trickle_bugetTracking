Trickle v12 — Design System

Everything a builder or tester needs to reproduce the prototype: the money ladder, colour, type, components, copy, notifications, and a five-student usability script. Specimens on this page are drawn by the app's own renderer.

Core unit

The dot ladder

One dot is always ₹100, on every tab. Bigger amounts fuse; smaller ones become a slice. The rule never changes by screen.

States

Rules

  • Order inside a visual: blocks, then pills, then dots, then the crumb. Σ mark value equals the amount to the rupee.
  • At most 30 marks per card (target about 20). Validator max on the build: see Phase 10 doc.
  • Tap a dot visual to zoom one level: blocks open into pills, pills into dots, with the exact ₹ caption.
  • The key ● = ₹100 appears once per screen with dots, plus in every tab intro. The full ladder key lives only in the Spending intro.
  • Size follows surface (P2-Q4 still open, built as rec A): widget 0.8×, card 1×, hero and zoom 1.25×. One constant, DENSITY, switches to a single size.

Colour

Palettes

Direction A, Monochrome glow. Hue lives only in the dots; surfaces are near-neutral. Appearance follows the phone. No red anywhere: spent is an outline, never a colour.

The dark CVD pair sits in the 6–8 floor band, which is legal only with secondary encoding. Trickle always shows jar names next to their dots, and savings sit in their own cards, so the pair is never colour-alone. Jar 4 and later reuse amber, blue and plum with a stripe or dot pattern and always show their name.

Black and white

Jars become grey with a pattern each (Food solid, Travel stripes, Fun dots); savings become full ink. App validator: 75 frames render in B&W light and dark with patterns, 0 red styles in 300 renders.

Glow

White glow (dark) or ink glow (light) marks time or position only: pace, today, next money in, due day, goal ETA, time of day, calendar. Never amounts.

Phase 12

Motion and sound

Calm everywhere, with springs only where the finger acts: pay, crumb snap, zoom, sheets and button press. The screen always renders its final state first; motion only plays from a start state into it, so a skipped animation never leaves anything half-drawn. Only transform, opacity and stroke-dashoffset move, and no moment animates more than 30 marks.

Sound rules

  • Coin only when money leaves or crumbs snap. Chimes only for good news: income, savings, goal milestones, month-end kept. Wooden clicks for interface touches. Charts, glow and story cards make no sound.
  • On by default and soft: each voice peaks at 0.15 or less, through a 5 kHz low-pass, compressor and limiter. All synthesised with WebAudio, no files.
  • Audio unlocks on the first tap. At most one sound every 300 ms; a more important sound ducks the earlier one to 30%.
  • Phone on silent means no sound; light haptics still play at pay, crumb snap, milestones and presses.

Settings and reduced motion

App settings → Sounds and motion: Sounds on/off, Haptics on/off, and Motion set to Follow phone (default), Reduce or Full. With reduced motion every moment shows its end state at once and no animation runs; sounds still play. Validator: 0 running animations under reduced motion, 0 stuck animations across all twelve moments.

Words

Copy rules

  1. Home shows no numbers. Pace is a word: Easy, Steady or Quick.
  2. At most two numbers on any first view. The bell log, All spends, the share poster and money lists are exempt.
  3. Never: over budget, overspent, deficit, debt, missed, streak, "you were gone", "only ₹X left". The validator greps for these.
  4. Bad news only where it can be acted on, with the action next to it ("Take ₹229 from Food?").
  5. Name money by where it is: left, spent, held, owed, kept. Owed money is never counted as yours.
  6. Equivalents use your own frequent buy ("about four dozen of your chais"), only inside Insights cards.
  7. No SMS. Money arrives from linked UPI IDs or is typed by hand; a bank statement import is a disabled placeholder.

Retention

Notification set (calm few)

At most one a day and three a week; none between 22:00 and 08:00. Each type has its own Android channel and one action. Simulated over 30 days: 9 sent, max 1/day, max 3/week, 0 in quiet hours.

Phase 11

Usability test: five students, 25 minutes each

Recruit five students in Chennai: two hostel, one day scholar, one renting, one earning. Android phone, prototype open at #fresh (tasks 2–3) and #demo (the rest). Think aloud; note time and taps. Pass bar per criterion is in the right column.

T1 · S1 S2Show a card with 3 pills, 4 dots and a slice for 5 seconds. "How much is this?" Then two jars side by side for 3 s: "Which has more left?" Pass: ≥4/5 say one dot = ₹100 within 5 s; ≥90% of comparisons right within 3 s.
T2 · S3"Set up Trickle for yourself." Stopwatch from the link screen to Home. Pass: ≤30 s, ≤4 taps with defaults (UPI path).
T3 · S3Repeat with "you don't use UPI apps". Pass: finishes the manual path with one typed amount; understands it can be changed later.
T4 · S5 S6 S13Home, 5 seconds, then hide. "How is your month going?" Pass: ≥4/5 answer with the pace word or the glow; nobody mentions a number.
T5 · S8"Pay for a chai." Pass: reaches UPI hand-off in ≤3 steps; notices the jar chip and "6th chai this week" without judging it.
T6 · S4 S11Pay ₹250 for a movie from Fun. Pass: understands the single "Take from Food?" question; nobody describes it as a warning or a mistake.
T7 · S9Trigger "Allowance arrives" and split it; then undo. Pass: one tap to split, finds Undo, says savings went first.
T8 · S12Split ₹1,200 dinner with three friends, then mark Kiran paid back. Pass: can say owed money is not theirs yet; sees dashed dots fill.
T9 · S7 S135-second tests on Spending, Savings and Insights. "What does this screen show?" Pass: ≥4/5 per screen; every tester names one dot as ₹100 without the key.
T10 · S10Open the weekly check-in and the September story. Pass: tester says they'd open it again; nobody reads it as a score.
T11 · a11ySwitch to black and white. "Which jar is Travel?" Pass: identified by pattern or name by ≥4/5.

Switch rule: if T1 comparison accuracy falls below 90% or median time exceeds 3 s, test a version with dots in fixed rows of five before changing the ladder.
