Identity and design system, v13

Built on Tarun’s own Figma colour scheme. Green to amber is the brand and the pace scale. His mesh gradients become a library of moments. Every rupee still draws as the same dot, now with depth.

Logo

The dot ladder, trickling down

The mark stacks a pill (₹1,000), a dot (₹100) and a half-dot crumb (₹50), the three smallest steps of the money ladder, read top to bottom like drops. The wordmark stays “Trickle” in Geist Bold at −4% tracking, as decided in v12.

Gradient library

Ten gradients, each tied to a moment

Taken from the three hero mesh gradients and the Glow and Dot-depth pairs in Tarun’s Color Scheme frame. A gradient only ever appears as a glow behind content, and its accent colours the key phrase and the button on that screen. Tide’s warm corner uses his “a bit fast” orange instead of coral, so no screen carries red.

Pace scale

Green when in step, amber when a bit fast

The Home glow and the Pace widget sit on one scale. Easy and Steady show Grove; Quick shows Ember. There is no red stop, because spending fast is a note, not a failure.

Dot system

One dot is always ₹100

The v12 ladder is unchanged. Marks now carry Tarun’s Dot-depth treatment: a light corner, the jar hue, and a darker corner.

crumb, less than ₹100, a wedge filled clockwise from 12

solid · money left
outline · spent

Components

Buttons, chips, tab bar, Pay

One primary button per screen. Inside the app the primary is ink; on onboarding and intro screens it takes the moment’s accent. Pay keeps the Grove gradient on every tab. Targets are at least 44px.

Onboarding and intro template

Glow, logo, two-line headline, one button

Based on the onboarding reference Tarun shared. The glow fills the top half and fades into the ground. Below it come the mark and wordmark, a two-line headline with the key phrase in the accent, a grey subline, step dots with the active one as a pill, a full-width accent button and a text link. Onboarding, the five tab intros and Welcome back all use it.
