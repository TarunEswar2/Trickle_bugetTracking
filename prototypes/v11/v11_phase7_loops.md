# Trickle v11: Phase 7, Loops, retention, anxiety, principles

## Diverge: loops and triggers
| Loop | Trigger | Action | Reward | Cost / risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily glance | habit, widget on phone | open Home | glow + word, savings tiles | numbers → anxiety (avoid) |
| Pay moment | UPI payment | pick category | tiles leave (friction), repeat line | nag if every pay prompts |
| Income day | credit arrives | Split it (1 tap) | savings tiles drop in + chime | none |
| Subscription day | due date | nothing (auto) | calm "Spotify paid" | surprise if unannounced → 1-day-before note |
| Weekly check-in | Sunday 7 pm | 4 cards | fresh start, this week vs last | skipped if ignored twice |
| Period-end story | last day | swipe 5 cards | "You saved ₹X" peak-end | long if >5 cards |
| Goal milestones | each 10% row | none | row fills, soft chime | spammy if every tile |
| Repeat buys | 3rd same payee in 7 days | see line at pay | awareness without judgement | could feel like shaming → neutral words |
| Friends pay back | UPI credit from split friend | auto match | dashed tiles fill | mis-match → ask once |
| Streaks / points | — | — | — | NEVER (what-the-hell effect) |

## First week journey
Day 0: splash → 4-step setup → Home shows savings tiles already dropped (endowed progress). Day 1: first pay moment, tiles leave, "That's how it works" tip once. Day 2: return trigger "Your first day in tiles" (1 push, 7 pm). Day 3: first repeat-buy line if applicable. Day 4–5: nothing (silence builds trust). Day 6: subscription reminder if due. Day 7: first weekly check-in ends with savings still intact.
Return triggers: day 2 (first-day recap), day 7 (check-in), day 30 (period story). Lapsed 5+ days: one gentle "Your savings are still here" with fresh-start framing; never a list of missed items.

## Notifications strategy
Caps: ≤1 per day, ≤4 per week. Quiet hours 22:00–08:00. Only: income split confirm, subscription 1 day before, weekly check-in, period story, lapsed fresh start. Never: overspend alerts as push, streak loss, numbers of remaining budget. No badge counts on app icon.

## Converge: loop map
Core (daily): Pay → tiles leave → Home glow → (next pay).
Monthly spine: Income day → auto-split → (subscriptions auto on due days) → weekly check-ins ×4 → period story → leftover to savings → fresh start → next income day.
Growth: savings rows → goal milestone → story peak-end.
Why: every loop ends on savings (peak-end), each has one action at most, and two loops (subscription day, leftover) need zero action.

## UX principles (14)
1. One decision per screen, default preselected (Fogg 2009; Madrian & Shea 2001).
2. ≤2 numbers on first view; exact ₹ on tap (Sweller 1988; NN/g progressive disclosure).
3. One unit forever: ■ = ₹100 (Neurath Isotype; Phase 3 test).
4. Home never shows budget or balance (Karlsson et al. 2009 ostrich effect).
5. Money arrives before it can be spent: savings first (Thaler & Benartzi 2004).
6. Feel the payment: tiles leave visibly (Prelec & Loewenstein 1998; Soman 2003).
7. Separate jars, flexible edges: move tiles, never break the total (Thaler 1999 mental accounting).
8. Start with progress already shown (Nunes & Drèze 2006) and speed up near the goal (Kivetz et al. 2006).
9. Weekly and monthly fresh starts (Dai, Milkman & Riis 2014).
10. End every period on the good number (Kahneman et al. 1993 peak-end).
11. No red, no debt words, no guilt (Elliot et al. 2007; Shapiro & Burchell 2012).
12. No streaks, points, badges or counts (Polivy & Herman; audit NEVER list).
13. Calm by default: ambient glow over alerts (Weiser & Brown 1996).
14. Your data stays yours: UPI link or manual only, no SMS, local-first.
