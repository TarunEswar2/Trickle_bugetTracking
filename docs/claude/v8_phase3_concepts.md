# Trickle v8 — Phase 3: Concepts

Artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/61viETnfoyFNFp5jJE1dEU ("Trickle v8 Concepts", switch A/B/C at top).
Same seed data for all: budget ₹6,000/month (Food 2,400 · Travel 900 · Fun 800 · Essentials 1,400), savings ₹4,200 toward Goa ₹8,000 (53%), allowance ₹9,000 → ₹6,000 budget + ₹3,000 savings. Pay demos: ₹180 chai (fits) and ₹600 movie night (over Fun's ₹350).
Rules held in every direction: no budget number on Home; glow green/amber never red; one choice per step; ≤2 numbers per screen; tabs Home | Money | Actions | Insights; no SMS.

## A · Jars (liquid)
- **Home glow:** bottom-up pool of light with a slow drifting wave. Pinned: Goa jar (53%), month jar tinted by pace.
- **Pay:** pick category → category jar; this payment is a slab of liquid that pours out. Over: jar empties and a dashed amber slab sits above the rim → "Take the rest from next week".
- **Money:** Allowance jar drains; Budget jar fills to its line, Savings jar rises. Confirm card "Got it" + Undo.
- Pros: most literal mental-accounting pot; pouring = strongest pain-of-paying cue; calm periphery glow. Cons: small payments barely move the level; many jars risk v7 clutter; overflow above the rim is least intuitive.

## B · Tiles (waffle)
- **Home glow:** blurred grid of tiles at the top. Pinned: 10×10 Goa waffle, 30-day month strip.
- **Pay:** category = 24 tiles; the tiles this payment uses highlight and pop out one by one. Over: all pop, amber dashed tiles appear below.
- **Money:** 18 allowance tiles (₹500 each) dissolve; 12 fill Budget, 6 fill Savings.
- Pros: countable without numbers; waffle = endowed-progress stamp card; pop sequence scales with payment size. Cons: most data-like; tile unit differs per category; glow weakest as pace signal.

## C · Orbs (rings)
- **Home glow:** one breathing orb at the top. Pinned: Goa ring (53%), month time-vs-spend double ring.
- **Pay:** category ring; this payment's arc lifts off and fades. Over: ring empties, dotted amber outer arc shows the overflow.
- **Money:** allowance orb splits into a Budget orb and a smaller Savings orb.
- Pros: purest ambient display (calm tech, Ambient Orb); glow carries all pace colour; goal-gradient ring closing. Cons: least literal for money; arcs hard to compare; echoes fitness "close your rings" gamification.

## Recommendation
A as the base (jars match the two-jar mental model and the "handing over cash" friction best), borrowing C's orb glow for Home and B's tiles for the Goa goal visual if the user wants countable progress.
